FPGA Segment and Demand:
- More professional and performance oriented - the artist will need to have a good knowledge of how the pedal works and spend some time to figure out how to get the most out of the product. They will be able to have a lot of control over their music, and will provide a product that is like being able to edit their music in post, but in real life.

DSP/Microcontroller Segment and Demand:
- More like a traditional pedal that has a little more capability than normal. They will be able to do all of the things that they want, but will need to have more know-how than normal, or just have to use the profiles that come automatically on the device
- Would be good to make this version more intuitive than the FPGA version, with some way to select different profile by turning a knob and having some tactile click that makes it clear which profile has been selected
	- Could have a 5 second "peak" switch that leads to the most extreme version of the filter so that the artist knows which profile they just switched to when they do. Its going to be hard to make this work without a screen. A screen wouldn't be the worst thing in the world - or even just a 7-seg display.
